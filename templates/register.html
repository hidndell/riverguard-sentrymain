Data Privacy Policy

Any SIM card numbers and optional personal details gathered through this registry are collected for the sole purpose of having data for the RiverGuard system in preparation for our Final Defense. Your information will be kept confidential and will be permanently destroyed once the researchers are allowed upon completion of the capstone project.
